# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers should:

- Discuss ideas and defects directly and professionally.
- Separate evidence from inference.
- Disclose uncertainty and limitations.
- Protect confidential and personal information.
- Credit prior work and contributors.
- Focus criticism on the design, evidence, or implementation rather than the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory conduct.
- Publishing another person's confidential information.
- Submitting credentials, client data, tenant identifiers, or real production configuration.
- Deliberately misrepresenting test results or capabilities.
- Removing attribution from contributed or adapted work.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile. The maintainer may edit, reject, lock, or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations.
